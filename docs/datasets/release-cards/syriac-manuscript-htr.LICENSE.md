# Licence: syriac-manuscript-htr

This dataset carries two parts under two different regimes. The split is deliberate: the annotation layer is the project's own work, while the page images reproduce a manuscript held in Alqosh.

## 1. Transcriptions, line polygons and baselines: CC BY 4.0

All transcriptions, line polygons, baselines and PAGE XML structure in this dataset are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit them as:

Dr. Nima Jamali, NOMOS project, LMU Munich

## 2. Page images: holding collection rights (Alqosh 65)

The seven page images of Alqosh 65 ship with this release by decision of the NOMOS project. They reproduce a nineteenth-century manuscript and remain subject to the rights of the holding collection, not under the CC licence above. The CC BY 4.0 licence covers the transcriptions, polygons and baselines only, never the images. Rights holders with a concern can reach the project through the contact section of the dataset card.
