# Licence: armenian-manuscript-htr

This dataset carries two parts under two different regimes. The split is deliberate: the annotation layer is the project's own work, while some page images reproduce holdings of the Bibliothèque nationale de France.

## 1. Transcriptions, line polygons and baselines: CC BY 4.0

All transcriptions, line polygons, baselines and PAGE XML structure in this dataset, for both manuscripts, are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit them as:

NOMOS project, LMU Munich

## 2. Page images: Gallica terms of use (BnF Arménien 172 only)

The Arménien 172 page images are digital reproductions served from Gallica (https://gallica.bnf.fr/), under Gallica's own conditions of use (https://gallica.bnf.fr/accueil/fr/html/conditions-dutilisation-de-gallica), not under the CC licence above. The relevant terms, as stated by the BnF:

"La réutilisation non commerciale de ces contenus est libre et gratuite dans le respect de la législation en vigueur et notamment du maintien de la mention de source" ("Source gallica.bnf.fr / Bibliothèque nationale de France").

Commercial reuse, including resale of the content or reuse generating direct revenue, is subject to payment and needs a licence (https://www.bnf.fr/fr/faire-une-utilisation-commerciale-dune-reproduction). Researchers are exempt from those fees for scientific publications. Every reuse of the images must carry the credit line:

Source gallica.bnf.fr / Bibliothèque nationale de France

Machine learning uses have their own clause. The use of Gallica documents and data "dans le cadre d'un projet en Intelligence artificielle (IA) ayant pour objet des traitements de données par l'IA (notamment la fouille de données) ou de l'IA générative" ("as part of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) project whose object is AI data processing (notably data mining) or generative AI") is "gratuite à des fins de recherche scientifique académique, à but non lucratif, sous réserve de la mention de source : BnF / Gallica" ("free for academic scientific research purposes, non-profit, subject to the source mention: BnF / Gallica") and "Payante pour les autres utilisations. Celles-ci font alors l'objet d'une autorisation et d'une licence." ("Paid for other uses. These then require an authorisation and a licence.", contact given by the BnF: prestations.numeriques@bnf.fr). Anyone may train on the transcriptions and geometry under CC BY 4.0, but training on the page images or line crops outside non-profit academic research needs a licence from the BnF. The AI-use credit reads:

BnF / Gallica

No images ship for UCLA Armenian MS 72, so this second part covers only the BnF manuscript.
